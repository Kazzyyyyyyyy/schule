Hello and welcome back to my podcast!

Today, I am talking about a topic that was requested in the comments and that is also quite close to me: **the music history and music scene of Karlsruhe, Germany.**

The city has something for any kind of person. Rock, punk, hardcore, hip-hop, jazz and electronic music all have their place in Karlsruhe. There are also many venues where local and international artists perform.

Some of the most important music venues are **Substage, JUBEZ and KOHI**. Substage, for example, was founded in 1990 by the Musikerinitiative Karlsruhe and originally focused strongly on providing a venue for rock bands. Today, its programme includes rock, hardcore, punk, blues, indie, pop and folk.

But Karlsruhe's rock history goes back even further. In the 1960s, the **Star-Club in Karlsruhe** became an important meeting place for the upcomming beat and rock scene.

So, Karlsruhe's connection to rock music is more than sixty years old. It has developed from the beat and rock scene of the 1960s into today's much wider alternative, rock and metal culture.

Another important part of the modern Karlsruhe music scene is **rap**.

One of the most well known rappers associated with Karlsruhe is **Haze**. He grew up in Karlsruhe and developed his musical identity there. He heavily focusses on Boom-Bap rap and 1990s hiphop. His first Releases Date back to 2012 with the Album Blues ausm Block. 

Boom Bap is a style of hip-hop that is strongly influenced by the sound of the 1990s. It often uses hard drums, strong basslines, samples and a darker atmosphere. Haze's music is especially known for its raw sound and stories about life in the city and on the streets. His 2018 album _Die Zwielicht LP_, for example, was described as dark and melancholic Boom Bap with heavy bass, drums and stories about difficult environments.

The lyrics are also an important part of this style.

Compared with some modern forms of German rap, traditional Boom Bap often puts more emphasis on complex rhyme schemes and detailed storytelling. Instead of focusing mainly on a catchy melody or a certain vibe, the rapper can use the lyrics to create a specific atmosphere and tell a story.

Haze has also been closely connected to **Alte Schule Records**, an independent record label associated with his music. The label released several of his albums, including _Guten Abend, Hip Hop..._ and _Die Zwielicht LP_. Other artists connected to this scene include **Svaba Ortak** and **MC Hero**, who has collaborated with Haze on more recent projects.

This shows an interesting connection between Karlsruhe's musical past and present day. The city has a history of adopting older musical styles and developing them further. 

For me, that is what makes Karlsruhe's music scene interesting. It is not based on just one genre. Instead, different generations and styles exist alongside each other. Old musical traditions are kept alive, while younger artists add their own ideas and modern influences.

And that is probably the most interesting thing about Karlsruhe: **the city does not simply forget its musical past. It takes old sounds, gives them a new identity and keeps them alive for the next generation.**
